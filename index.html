Data Contracts Formal schema & SLA agreements between data producers and consumers — pioneered by Uber, LinkedIn & Spotify
Schema Contract
Enforces field names, types & required columns at the Bronze boundary. Prevents breaking changes reaching downstream.
SLA Contract
Defines freshness, latency & availability guarantees. Consumers know when data arrives and how stale it can be.
Quality Contract
Null rates, referential integrity & value distribution checks — producers are accountable for the data they publish.
Ownership Contract
Named domain owner, team Slack channel, on-call runbook & incident escalation path for every data product.
Medallion Architecture
Industry-standard data design pattern for Lakehouse systems — progressively refine data across three logical layers
Bronze — Raw Layer
Data ingested as-is from source systems with no transformation. Acts as the system of record and enables full replay.
Raw Ingestion Full Fidelity Append-Only Schema-on-Read Audit & Replay
▶
Silver — Refined Layer (Source Data Products)
Validated, deduplicated, and conformed data. Applies business rules, type casting, and joins for a clean enterprise view.
Cleansed Deduplicated Conformed Business Rules Joined & Enriched
▶
Gold — Curated Layer
Aggregated, domain-specific datasets optimised for analytics, BI, and ML. Delivered as data products to consumers.
Aggregated BI-Ready ML Features Data Products SLA-Governed
▶
Platinum — DDL Layer
Derived Data Products built on top of curated Gold outputs. Cross-domain, heavily aggregated datasets packaged as reusable, governed data products for enterprise-wide consumption.
